Before you submit

Prepare the work
behind the application.

  1. Confirm degree recognition and any subject or credit requirements.
  2. Check accepted language certificates and their validity.
  3. Tailor your portfolio to the programme’s project, page and file-size limits.
  4. Confirm the current deadline and full cost, including materials and living expenses.

Open the portfolio studio ↗

University submissions follow each institution’s instructions. A website does not replace a required admissions PDF.

This is a researched shortlist, not a complete directory of every European programme. Some entries require additional language study, degree-equivalence assessment or substantial funding. The Paris entry is a placement pathway, not guaranteed direct entry into a two-year master’s. Applications are completed with the universities through the official links above.
